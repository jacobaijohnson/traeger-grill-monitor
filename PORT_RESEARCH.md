# Port Selection: 8420

Port 8420 is confirmed **Unassigned** by IANA (range 8418-8422 is all unassigned).
SpeedGuide.net confirms no known services, vulnerabilities, or applications use this port.

## Why 8420?

- IANA Unassigned (verified via SpeedGuide.net and IANA registry)
- Not used by any common self-hosted/homelab application
- "420" has a fun BBQ/smoke association
- Not in the heavily contested 8000-8100 or 8900-9100 ranges
- Easy to remember
